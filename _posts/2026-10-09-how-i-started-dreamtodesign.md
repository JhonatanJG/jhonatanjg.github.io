title: "How I Started My Online Business: DreamToDesign"
---

A few months ago, my partner showed me an idea she had seen online:
selling digital products to generate extra income. I loved it right
away, because digital products don't need inventory and can be sold
again and again.

I started by researching the platforms available on the market,
Shopify among them. Instead of committing for a full year, we chose to
test Shopify with its three-month trial for just one dollar. That way
we could find out how much potential the business really had before
investing more.

Then the real work began. I built and edited the store, and I learned
how online payments work and how to deliver the files to customers by
email. Meanwhile, my partner designed the templates: birthday
invitations, event cards and designs for every season, from trending
characters to F1 cars.

The best part was learning by doing. Every small problem, from a
payment setup to a confusing store layout, taught me something about
how the digital economy works. DreamToDesign is still growing, but it
has already shown me that anyone with a good idea and some curiosity
can start an online business.
